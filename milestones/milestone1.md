## Reflection

### 1. What went well during this unit?

I think the brainstorming went really well. I had two ideas going into this and I was able to actually think through which one made sense for a relational database project versus which one was really an AI project in disguise. Once I landed on the study planner idea, the schema pretty much fell into place on its own — the one-to-many, one-to-one, and many-to-many relationships weren't things I had to force in to check a box, they came out of how the data actually needed to work. I also liked that I caught the "weekly goal vs backlog" confusion before I built anything around it, instead of realizing it later once I already had code written.

### 2. What were some challenges your group faced in this unit?

Since I'm working on this solo with an AI agent instead of a real team, the challenge wasn't splitting up work or coordinating schedules — it was that every decision landed on me. I had to be the one to catch when something didn't make sense, like figuring out where a skipped task should actually go, or deciding if "weekly goal" needed its own table or not. It would've been easy to just accept a plausible-sounding answer and move on, so I had to keep pushing back on my own ideas and asking "wait, does this actually work" instead of assuming it was fine.

### 3. What additional support will you need in upcoming units as you continue to work on your final project?

I think I'll need the most help once I actually start building the plan-generation algorithm — that's the part of the app with real logic behind it, not just CRUD, so I want to make sure I don't either oversimplify it or over-engineer it. I'd also like some feedback once I build out the ERD in Milestone 2, just to sanity check that the schema is still the right amount of complexity for the timeline I have. And since the OAuth and sessions concept from the labs was the hardest one for me to fully get, I'll probably need to slow down and go over that again carefully when I get to that part instead of rushing through it.
